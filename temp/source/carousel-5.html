Get started

Set up once, then it's one click.

1

Add FigBlend to Blender

Free add-on for Blender 4.2 or newer. Add its repository and Blender keeps it up to date.

2

Start Live Link

Save your .blend, open the FigBlend tab in the sidebar and press Start Live Link.

3

Send from Figma

Select a frame, pick a mode, press Send to Blender. It arrives in seconds.

Your design never leaves your computer. Live Link talks only to Blender on localhost.
Add-on and setup: github.com/explainervid-glitch/figblend
